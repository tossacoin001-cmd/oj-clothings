The Process

Four steps to perfect.

Simple, personal, and entirely focused on getting it right.

01

Consult

We discuss your occasion, style preferences, and fabric options. In-store or via WhatsApp — we meet you where you are.

02

Measure

Precise measurements taken in-store at either Lekki location, or guided virtually through our WhatsApp fitting process.

03

Craft

Our tailors cut, sew, and hand-finish every piece. Premium damask, aso-oke, satin, suede — chosen and crafted with care.

04

Deliver

Your piece arrives ready to wear — packaged with care. Lagos delivery in 24–48hrs. International shipping worldwide.
